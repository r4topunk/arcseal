Sealed voting for Arc

ArcSeal

Votes nobody can read until the round closes — and anyone can reveal after.

See how it works View on GitHub
The problem
Public voting has a bandwagon problem: a running tally shapes votes before they are cast, and every vote is readable onchain the moment it lands.
The solution
Every vote is timelock-encrypted to a drand round chosen when voting opens. Nobody, including the contract, can read it before that round is public — and after that, anyone can decrypt it and reveal it in one transaction.

How it works

Timelock encryption first, as a sealed envelope — then exactly how SealedDAO uses it for a vote.

A sealed envelope that opens itself

You write your choice, seal it, and hand it over in public. Anyone can see the envelope exists — nobody can see what's inside. When the clock you agreed on arrives, the lock opens on its own. Nobody has to come back and unlock it.

  1. 1Seal — encrypt your choice to a moment in the future.
  2. 2Wait — that moment arrives, on a public clock nobody controls.
  3. 3Open — anyone can decrypt it and check it against your fingerprint.

How SealedDAO voting works

  1. ProposeA member proposes a USDC transfer or a membership change, and picks a voting window from 10 minutes to 7 days.
  2. SealEach member seals one vote — For, Against or Abstain — before the window closes. Only a hash and an encrypted blob are ever posted.
  3. CloseVoting closes at a chosen drand round. From that instant, anyone can decrypt every sealed vote.
  4. RevealAnyone decrypts every vote in their own browser and submits them in one transaction. When the proposal met quorum, a small fixed payment per revealed vote covers that transaction's gas.
  5. Finalize24 hours after the close, the tally is fixed: quorum is measured over sealed votes, and passing is measured over revealed votes.
  6. Execute & claimA passed proposal can be executed within 7 days after the reveal window ends. Every payout is claimed by its own recipient — never pushed to them.

Privacy, stated plainly

  • Secrecy holds during voting. Nobody — not other voters, not the contract, not ArcSeal's authors — can read a sealed vote's choice before its round is public. This relies on drand's League of Entropy threshold staying honest.
  • From the close round on, anyone can decrypt every sealed vote from the chain; after the reveal, each vote is public per address, permanently, in the event log.
  • This is not anonymity. If you need who voted, or how, to stay hidden after the fact, ArcSeal is not the right tool.
  • It does not stop a willing seller. Your vote receipt proves your choice to anyone you show it to, so ArcSeal is not coercion-resistant.
